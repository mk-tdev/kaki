# Product and pitch brief

## The promise

KAKI makes asking easier, helping smaller, and community impact visible.

> Singapore has apps for services. Pek Kio needs a way for neighbours to become each other’s support system.

## Primary users

- **Resident:** needs occasional practical help, company or confidence; can also share valuable skills and stories.
- **Helper:** wants meaningful volunteering that fits into 15–45 minute pockets of time.
- **Organiser:** needs safe coordination, escalation visibility and credible outcome reporting.

## The full loop

1. A resident speaks or types a need.
2. AI structures it into a plain-language mission and safety level.
3. A helper discovers and accepts the mission.
4. Both receive location, language and a three-step guide.
5. They coordinate and complete the interaction.
6. With consent, the moment becomes a bloom and updates community metrics.

## Impact measurement

- Completed neighbour moments
- Minutes of real-world connection
- Repeat matches within 60 days
- Skills exchanged in both directions
- Repaired or shared items kept in use
- Requests escalated and followed up by organisers

The pilot target is 100 completed missions in three months, with at least 30% of first interactions leading to a repeat connection.

## 90-second demonstration

1. Auntie Mei says: “I want to learn how to send photos to my grandson.”
2. KAKI produces a safe 20-minute digital-help mission and a simple guide.
3. Arjun accepts it and sees why he matched.
4. Mark the mission complete and add: “Auntie Mei sent her first family photo all by herself.”
5. Open Kampung Bloom. The counter increases and the new story appears.

## Judging alignment

- **Problem and impact:** ageing, social isolation, digital inclusion and practical intergenerational connection are addressed through measurable offline interactions.
- **Innovation and solution:** AI translates unstructured multilingual needs into safe, feasible micro-missions and explainable matches; it does not pretend to replace human care.
- **Prototype and pitch:** the end-to-end workflow is functional, visually distinctive, installable, backend-ready and demonstrable in under two minutes.
